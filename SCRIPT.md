# Hermes Digital Twin - Showcase Script (90 Seconds)

**Word Count:** ~240 words
**Estimated Pace:** Normal, conversational (approx. 1.5 minutes)

---

### 0:00 - Introduction & Baseline
*(Setup: Ensure you are on the **Operator Dashboard**. Press **`Shift + 0`** before hitting record to set the normal state).*

**Speaker:** 
"Welcome to Hermes, our digital twin platform for predictive aircraft maintenance. Right now, we're looking at the Operator Dashboard during a standard flight. Everything is nominal—Engine Health is a solid 98%, and oil pressure is perfectly stable at 65 PSI."

### 0:15 - The Degradation Begins
*(Action: Press **`Shift + 1`**)*

**Speaker:** 
"But watch what happens here. Our digital twin detects a subtle thermodynamic mismatch. You can see the engine health and oil pressure slowly starting to drop, immediately triggering a system warning. This isn't a hard physical failure yet, but Hermes sees the trend."

### 0:25 - Critical Escalation
*(Action: Press **`Shift + 2`**)*

**Speaker:** 
"Seconds later, the degradation accelerates. The pressure plummets down to 40 PSI, and a Critical Anomaly is flagged. The operator knows the aircraft is in danger, but they need to know exactly *why*."

### 0:35 - Engineer Diagnosis
*(Action: Navigate to **Engineer Dashboard**. Press **`Shift + 3`**)*

**Speaker:** 
"Let's switch to the Engineer's view. Hermes' machine learning models have already diagnosed the root cause. The Fault Matrix confirms an 88% probability of Oil Starvation. And you can clearly see on the Residual Chart how the actual oil pressure has completely deviated from our digital twin's expected baseline."

### 0:55 - Mission Sandbox Mitigation
*(Action: Scroll to Mission Sandbox. Press **`Shift + 4`**)*

**Speaker:** 
"We need a solution immediately, so we use the predictive Mission Sandbox. By simulating a drop in altitude to 10,000 feet and reducing the engine load to 60%..."

*(Action: Drag Altitude slider to 10,000 and Engine Load to 60%. Click **CALCULATE MITIGATION STRATEGY**)*

**Speaker:** 
"...Hermes predicts we can drop the mission risk to just 12% and safely extend our Remaining Useful Life by over an hour. We push this strategy straight to the operator."

*(Action: Click **PUSH TO OPERATOR** — Note: This automatically sets Scene 5 behind the scenes).*

### 1:15 - Operator Execution
*(Action: Navigate back to **Operator Dashboard**)*

**Speaker:** 
"Back in the cockpit, the operator receives the Engineer Advisory banner. They accept the new flight profile..."

*(Action: Click **ACCEPT** on the blue banner. Wait a second for values to stabilize)*

**Speaker:** 
"...and the engine instantly stabilizes. We've successfully averted a mid-air failure."

### 1:25 - Maintenance Integration
*(Action: Navigate to **Maintenance Dashboard**. Press **`Shift + 6`**)*

**Speaker:** 
"Finally, when the aircraft lands, the maintenance crew doesn't have to guess what went wrong. They already have an A-Level priority diagnosis for Oil Pump Degradation, backed by all the sensor evidence from the flight."

*(Action: Hover over the **ACKNOWLEDGE & GENERATE WORK ORDER** button)*

**Speaker:** 
"This is how Hermes bridges operations, engineering, and maintenance into one proactive ecosystem. Thank you."
