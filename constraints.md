### Mission Constraints for Digital Twin 
| **Minimum safe altitude / terrain clearance** | Prevent controlled flight into terrain |
| **Maximum/minimum operating altitude** | Engine + aircraft performance limits |
| **Engine operating limits** | RPM, CHT, oil pressure, temperature, etc. |
| **Fuel/endurance** | Can the UAV reach RTB/alternate? |
| **Weather / icing** | Can make an otherwise valid trajectory unsafe |
| **Link status** | May trigger different recovery procedures |
| **Airspace/geographic restrictions** | UAV may not be allowed to divert anywhere |
| **Mission objective** | ISR target/area may make some deviations undesirable |
| **Emergency landing options** | If RTB becomes impossible |
| **Remaining RUL / degradation rate** | How aggressively should the mission be terminated? |
